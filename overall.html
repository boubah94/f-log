😴 Sleep — 7h26m average, a real step up (with an asterisk)

Four nights tracked (mornings Oct 27–30) averaged 7h26m asleep with 1h32m deep. Only one night fell under 6 hours (5h04m); the other three ran 7h46m–8h44m. That's a genuine jump from the 5½-hour weeknights the last two editions flagged — the JAMA (2011) finding stands: a week of 5-hour nights cuts testosterone 10–15% in young men, and most growth hormone arrives in deep sleep.

So what: direction: fixed. Asterisk: two nights never got tracked and wake times ran post-shift (10 AM–1:38 PM), so this is a partial picture, not a settled trend. The win now is making 7+ hours boring and repeatable, not heroic.
